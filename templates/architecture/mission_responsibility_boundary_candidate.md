# Mission Responsibility Boundary Candidate

Status: template candidate — non-executable.

This template records whether a requested answer, draft, review or external-facing wording may exceed the architect's mission or imply unwanted responsibility.

It is not legal advice, insurance confirmation, contract validation, approval, email sending, Notion write or professional validation.

## User posture question

Use only if the user has not explicitly requested a mail or posture.

```text
Do you want:
1. a short reply saying we cannot pronounce;
2. a bounded reply with points of vigilance and referral to the competent party;
3. a reply proposing a mission complement or specialist review?
```

## Boundary

```text
A useful answer must not become an unintended mission extension.
If the subject is outside mission, the safest reply is a boundary reply.
Guidance remains orientation and referral, not prescription or validation.
```
