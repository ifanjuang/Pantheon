# Anonymized Role Drift Evidence Tree Candidate

Status: example — candidate output, documented non-implemented.

This example abstracts patterns from ChatGPT project-context discussions. It intentionally avoids real names, addresses, private emails and project identifiers.

It is not a project record, not proof, not a legal position and not an external communication.

## 1. Question instructed

```text
question:
  Is the situation beginning to drift from normal MOE assistance into role confusion, implicit approval or mission extension?

professional_act:
  early warning + source completion + role reminder candidate

source_basis:
  chatgpt_project_context_candidate

output_status:
  evidence_tree_candidate + output_structure_candidate
```

## 2. Candidate claims

| Claim | Source support | Role reading | Risk | Required gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project owner has begun direct exchanges with contractors while expecting MOE validation. | ChatGPT project-context pattern. | Project-owner decision and MOE advice may blur. | Medium to high. | Source completion. |
| Contractor-side incomplete works or unclear extras may be mixed with agency fee/payment disputes. | ChatGPT project-context pattern. | Payment, reserve and responsibility channels may blur. | High. | Human review. |
| Replacement or corrective works may need a distinct scope and possible amendment. | ChatGPT project-context pattern + MAF perimeter caution. | Continuing as if mission unchanged may create scope drift. | High. | Mission-scope review. |
| Reception, reserve lifting and payment must be separated. | Professional-risk review layer. | Reception is a project-owner decision assisted by MOE, not an automatic consequence of payment or follow-up. | High. | Pre-reception source pack. |
| Full synthesis or detailed new quote review should not be assumed without mission basis. | Professional-risk review layer. | Additional service may require explicit scope. | Medium to high. | Contract / amendment check. |

## 3. Responsibility chain candidate

```text
project_owner:
  decides, arbitrates, signs markets, approves quotes, decides reception and payment.

architect_moe:
  assists, alerts, coordinates information, checks coherence within mission scope, prepares observations and candidates.

contractor:
  prices, verifies existing conditions, performs works, corrects its lot, answers on scope and schedule.

amo_or_finance_actor:
  may inform funding or coherence constraints, but does not replace MOE mission scope.

replacement_contractor:
  must be scoped separately; corrective works must not blur prior contractor responsibilities.
```

## 4. Tensions

```text
- client urgency vs source-based review;
- direct contractor instruction vs MOE coordination;
- quote modification vs formal validation;
- open items vs payment pressure;
- reception preparation vs reserve closure;
- corrective works vs mission amendment;
- professional-risk signals vs normal correspondence.
```
