# Drawing reading checklist

Load only the part matching the drawing's nature and the question.

## Plan de niveau (floor plan)

- Level and its altimetry; north arrow; grid lines both ways.
- Rooms: name, number, area; tables of surfaces if present.
- Exterior walls against partitions (thickness, hatch); load-bearing walls when
  marked; existing / demolished / new by legend.
- Openings: symbols (door swing, sliding, window, French window), marks,
  widths written on the plan or in a schedule.
- Stairs (direction arrow, number of risers), voids, shafts.
- Section marks pointing to sections; detail callouts pointing to details.

## Coupe (section)

- Section mark it belongs to (A-A), and where it cuts on the plan.
- Levels and altimetry; floor-to-floor and clear heights.
- Sills, lintels, opening heights; roof slopes; foundations.
- Layers of walls, floors and roofs when drawn.

## Façade / élévation

- Which side: orientation (nord, sud…) or named side (rue, jardin). An
  orientation stated on the drawing is read; one inferred from the plan is
  deduced and depends on the north arrow.
- Openings as seen from outside: count, alignment, sills; materials and
  finishes by legend; heights (ridge, eaves, acrotère).

## Détail

- Its subject (junction, threshold, roof edge…) and where it applies (callout
  on plan or section). A detail may concern several elements: do not force it
  onto one room or wall.
- Materials, thicknesses, fixing; quote notes as written.

## Plan masse / situation

- Plot limits, setbacks, access, orientation, levels of the ground.

## Structure (engineer's drawings)

- Grid lines (same names as the architect's when coordinated; say when they
  differ), columns and beams with their marks and sections, slabs and their
  thickness, foundations, reinforcement notes.
- Element marks link to schedules or notes on the same drawing.

## Tables found on drawings

Surface tables, door and window schedules, finish schedules, legends of
symbols, revision tables (index, date, subject of each change). A revision
table says what changed at each index: quote it when asked what changed on a
drawing.
