# Hercule — distillation poster

Status: support documentaire, non canonique, non executable.

Portee : distillation visuelle pour une planche pedagogique autonome consacree aux travaux d'Hercule et aux disciplines d'usage de l'IA.

Ce fichier prepare une affiche, un poster, un cartel visuel ou une planche HTML.

Il ne remplace pas `hercule_ai_discipline.md`, `hercule_reconciliation_matrix.md` ni les douze `*_scene_card.md`.

Il ne definit aucun runtime, aucun agent, aucun outil, aucun workflow executable, aucune politique d'approbation et aucune memoire canonique.

Doctrine Pantheon preservee :

```text
OpenWebUI expose.
Hermes Agent execute.
Pantheon Next gouverne.
```

## Intention de la planche

La planche Hercule doit etre plus directe que la planche Ulysse.

Ulysse apprend a reconnaitre ce qui egare.

Hercule apprend a transformer une puissance en travail fiable.

La planche ne doit donc pas seulement dire : controler l'IA.

Elle doit montrer douze gestes concrets : tester, choisir, formuler, cadrer, nettoyer, discerner, encadrer, arreter, autoriser, organiser, murir, garder.

```text
Ulysse = deceler les pieges.
Hercule = maitriser les pouvoirs.
Pantheon = gouverner les statuts.
```

Le lecteur doit comprendre chaque scene sans connaitre la legende.

Regle poster :

```text
Mythe en repere.
Usage IA en clair.
Geste humain en action.
```

## Architecture de la planche

Format recommande : horizontal UHD.

Composition : un territoire structure en douze stations, ateliers, seuils et dispositifs de maitrise.

La planche Hercule doit etre plus terrestre, plus construite, plus atelier que la planche Ulysse.

Structure :

```text
haut : titre, sous-titre, phrase centrale ;
centre : douze stations reliees par chemins, seuils, flux et attelages ;
bas : regle de responsabilite ;
coin discret : statut documentaire, non executable.
```

La mythologie doit guider l'image.

Le texte doit parler de l'IA.

## Prompt image — version principale

```text
Create a large UHD horizontal illustrated educational poster in an abstract isometric editorial style.

Title: HERCULE — MAITRISER LES POUVOIRS
Subtitle: Douze gestes pour transformer la puissance de l'IA en travail fiable.
Central phrase: Ce que l'IA peut faire n'est pas encore ce qu'elle doit faire.

The poster is a conceptual landscape of twelve disciplined labors about AI literacy.
It is not an ancient parchment map and not a literal Greek historical scene.
It should feel timeless, architectural, structured, symbolic, rigorous and readable.

Show twelve distinct stations or workshops connected through paths, thresholds, channels or structured flows.
Each station represents one concrete gesture for using AI responsibly:
1 Lion — testing what impresses: AI power must hold in real cases.
2 Hydra — multiplying then choosing: one more option can be one less decision.
3 Deer — grasping true intention: a good prompt begins with what must not be lost.
4 Boar — framing disorder: coherent synthesis can hide a badly framed case.
5 Augean stables — cleaning before producing: a clean answer can hide a dirty corpus.
6 Stymphalian birds — making noise visible: what occupies attention does not always clarify decision.
7 Cretan bull — framing action: an available tool is not a right to act.
8 Mares — stopping autonomy: what continues alone must know when to return control.
9 Hippolyta's belt — authorizing circulation: readable data is not automatically transmissible data.
10 Geryon's cattle — organizing forces: models, sources, tools, memory and humans need roles and status.
11 Golden apples — maturing deliverables: deliver only what can be verified, carried and assumed.
12 Cerberus — guarding return: everything that remains may return with the wrong status.

Use symbolic visual metaphors:
- a lion transformed into a protective skin after a real test;
- multi-headed options being selected and cauterized;
- a fragile deer-like thread of intention held gently;
- a wild boar brought into a readable frame;
- rivers washing dirty documents, duplicates and versions;
- a flock of metallic birds revealed by resonance;
- a bull contained by explicit thresholds, permissions and validation;
- autonomous horses stopped by a clear boundary;
- a belt passing through a mandate gate;
- an attelage linking tools, people, sources and roles;
- golden fruits weighed before delivery;
- a guardian at the gate of memory and return.

Visual style:
abstract isometric illustration, clean outlined shapes, simplified characters, diagram-like worldbuilding, flat to lightly shaded color areas, editorial drawing feel, symbolic but readable.

Avoid cyberpunk, realistic robots, ancient parchment, literal Greek costumes, photorealism, corporate SaaS dashboards, dense text.

Keep text large and minimal.
The poster must be readable at a glance.
```
